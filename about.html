Who we are

A parish with a place for you

Our parish brings together St Michael and All Angels in Mickleham and the Chapel of Ease in Westhumble. We are part of the Church of England and the United Benefice of Leatherhead and Mickleham, within the Diocese of Guildford.

People from the villages and nearby towns form a friendly church community. We seek to care for one another through worship, spiritual support, practical help and encouragement. All are welcome to visit or join in parish life.
