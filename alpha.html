Alpha is a series of sessions exploring the basics of Christian faith. It is an opportunity to ask questions and share your point of view, whatever your background or context.

You do not need to have everything worked out before you come. Bring your curiosity, your questions and yourself.

Conversation, connection & discovery

What happens at a session?

The parish’s traditional format brings three simple ingredients together.

  1. Tea & cake

    A friendly beginning, with time to meet and get to know one another.

  2. A short film

    A presentation exploring a question about Christian faith.

  3. Time to talk

    A chance to ask questions, listen and share your own thoughts.
