Alpha Course

A welcoming space to explore the basics of Christian faith, ask questions and share your point of view.

What is Alpha?

The parish describes Alpha as a series of sessions exploring the basics of Christian faith. It is for anyone who would like to explore, ask questions and share their point of view, whatever their background or context.

What happens at a session?

The format described by the parish begins with tea and cake, followed by a short film presentation on a question about Christian faith and time for discussion. The course material explores topics including Jesus, prayer, faith, the Holy Spirit, the Bible and how God guides us.

Who is Alpha for?

Alpha is intended for people who want space to explore Christian faith and ask questions. You do not need to have everything worked out before you come.
