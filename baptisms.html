Baptisms

Baptism welcomes children and adults into the Christian faith and the life of the church.

A welcome into the church

The parish’s previous information welcomed children and adults to consider baptism, also called christening, as a way of entering the Christian faith. It invited people who live in the parish or have a strong connection with the church to make contact.

The earlier page described baptisms within a Sunday service. Please contact the church to confirm current arrangements, preparation and availability.

Start a conversation

If you are thinking about baptism for yourself or a child, the parish can explain the next steps and answer your questions.
