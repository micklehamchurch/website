Bible Study & Fellowship

The parish’s fellowship groups make space for Bible study, honest conversation, friendship and spiritual growth.

Learning and growing together

The parish describes fellowship and Bible study groups as an important part of church life. Their purpose is to make room for personal discovery and spiritual growth as people learn, ask questions and share the joys and challenges of Christian life.

The previous website described small groups meeting in Westhumble, with weekly gatherings at specific times. Those times and contacts may be out of date, so please ask the parish about current groups before making plans.

Everyone is welcome

These groups were described as informal and welcoming, with time for conversation and refreshments. Contact the parish to ask whether a group is currently meeting and whether it would suit you.
