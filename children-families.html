A place to grow together

Children are welcome here

The parish seeks to encourage children and young people to develop their own Christian faith, while supporting parents and families as they learn about God. The hope is that each person can grow in confidence, discover the gifts God has given them and find support for life’s challenges.

✝

Children, students and young families are invited to share in a loving church community, where there is room for fun as well as learning about God.
