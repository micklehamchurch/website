The Church Building

Explore the historic fabric of St Michael and All Angels through the parish’s account of the building.

Historic fabric

The parish history describes a church with early Saxon traces and substantial Norman and medieval fabric. Among the features it records are the chancel arch, chancel lancet windows, stone font, Norbury Chapel, medieval tombstones, carved pulpit and six bells.

The building has been altered over the centuries. Read the fuller Our History account for the parish’s notes on restorations and historic features. Please confirm current opening and visitor arrangements before making a special journey.
