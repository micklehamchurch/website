The Churchyard

The churchyard is a place of remembrance and a part of Mickleham’s local history.

A place of remembrance

The old parish website describes the churchyard as a place of beauty and historical value and points to a guide to its oldest part. It also publishes detailed churchyard regulations covering burials, memorials, flowers and the Book of Remembrance.

Those regulations were stated as approved by the PCC on 7 November 2023. Because local rules and availability can change, contact the parish for the current regulations, forms and advice before arranging a memorial, planting or burial.
