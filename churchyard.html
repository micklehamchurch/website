The Churchyard

The churchyard is a place of remembrance and a part of Mickleham’s local history.

A place of remembrance

For generations, the churchyard at St Michael & All Angels has been a place of remembrance, reflection and quiet beauty.

Surrounding the church are memorials to people whose lives form part of the story of Mickleham. Some stones have stood here for centuries, while others remember more recent generations. Together they give the churchyard a special connection between the parish today and those who came before us.

Set among mature trees and with the Surrey countryside all around, it is also a peaceful place simply to walk, pause and reflect. Visitors are very welcome to explore the churchyard and discover this beautiful part of our parish.

For information about memorials, burials or the churchyard regulations, please contact the parish or read the current regulations.

Exploring the churchyard’s history

The parish’s historical account records a Guide to the Oldest Part of the Churchyard, compiled by Carole Brough Fuller. The complete guide is now available to read below, as a preserved source for exploring the people and memorials remembered here.

The Mickleham & Westhumble Local History Group preserves local photographs, stories and research. Its independent website is a useful starting point for enquiries about parish and village history. For wildlife and care of the grounds, the Eco Church page includes the locally preserved 2024 Churchyard Trail.
