About our parish

The Electoral Roll

The Electoral Roll is the parish’s formal list of lay members of the Church of England who meet the eligibility requirements.

What it is

Each Church of England parish maintains an Electoral Roll. Being on the roll records a person’s connection with the parish and allows them to take part in the parish’s governance, subject to the Church’s rules.

Who can apply

Eligibility depends on the Church of England’s current rules, including age, baptism and a qualifying connection with the parish or regular worship. Please ask the Electoral Roll Officer or parish for the current application form and guidance.

Applications and privacy

The previous website named an Electoral Roll Officer and included information connected to a past APCM. Dates and contact details may be out of date, so no deadline or named officer is published here. Contact the parish to ask how to apply and how Electoral Roll information is handled.
