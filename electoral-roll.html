About our parish

Electoral Roll

The Church Electoral Roll is the parish’s formal list of lay members. It records a person’s connection with the parish and enables participation in parish governance.

What is the Church Electoral Roll?

Each Church of England parish maintains an Electoral Roll. Being on the roll records a person’s connection with the parish and enables participation in parish governance, subject to the Church Representation Rules.

Why join the Electoral Roll?

Membership gives a formal place in the life of the parish. It enables participation in the Annual Parochial Church Meeting and, where applicable under the Church Representation Rules, elections to the Parochial Church Council and wider Church synods. The application form explains the declarations required.

How to join

Download and complete the application form below. The form includes the declarations and notes needed to apply for inclusion on the parish Electoral Roll.
