Find Us

St Michael and All Angels is on Old London Road in Mickleham; Westhumble Chapel is on Crabtree Lane.

St Michael and All Angels, Mickleham

Old London Road, Mickleham, Dorking, Surrey RH5 6DU, England.

The old parish visitor information describes a bike rack by the main entrance and the 465 bus stopping outside the church. It also says Box Hill & Westhumble station is about 1.4 miles away, a walk of around 30 minutes. Bus routes, facilities and journey times can change; check current travel information before setting out.

Chapel of Ease, Westhumble

Crabtree Lane, Westhumble, Surrey RH5 6BG. The previous parish website gives this address. Please contact the parish if you need help confirming the precise entrance or current access arrangements.
