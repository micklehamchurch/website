Funerals and remembrance

A place to begin when you are arranging a funeral or seeking prayer and support.

With care and compassion

The parish marks significant occasions, including funerals, for members of the church and the wider community. If someone has died, you may speak with your funeral director or contact the parish to ask about a service and the support available.

Arrangements depend on current availability and circumstances. The parish can discuss the options with you.
