1086

A church in Domesday

A written record of a church at Mickleham, alongside two acres of meadow.

In 1086, the Domesday survey recorded a church at Mickleham. The parish history preserves this short English rendering of the entry:

“There is a Church and two acres of meadow.”

Those few words are a documentary anchor for the parish’s history: a church existed here by the time of the survey. They do not tell us when it was founded or how its walls, roof and windows looked.

The local history group also reproduces the entry in its account of the church’s earliest years. The medieval building we see today belongs to several later chapters as well as this early beginning.
