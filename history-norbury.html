c. 1300

The Norbury Chapel

A medieval side chapel with a stone tomb, memorial brasses and connections to Norbury families.

The side chapel dates from around 1300. Its chequered flint and stone distinguish it from the surrounding church, giving this small space a character of its own.

Inside is the chest tomb of William Wyddowsoun and his wife, with brass figures and an inscription above it. William is remembered as a London citizen and mercer. The tomb’s decorated panels and the figures preserve a personal story within the building’s longer history.

Families remembered here

  • The parish account links the chapel to the Stydolf family and, later, to the Locks of Norbury, who used it as a family pew.

The memorial belongs to the early 16th century. Sources differ over its exact year, so no single year is given here.
