The side chapel, now known as the Norbury Chapel, is described as dating from around 1300. It forms part of the medieval fabric of St Michael and All Angels.

The chapel contains the Wyddowsoun tomb and brasses recorded in the church’s history. These memorials are among the historic features preserved within the building.
