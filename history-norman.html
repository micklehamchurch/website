c. 1180

Norman work

Much of the present church dates to the Norman period, around 1180, including the chancel arch, lancet windows and font.

Much of the present church is dated to the Norman period, around 1180. The chancel arch, chancel lancet windows and stone font are among the early features recorded in its history.

The chancel arch is described as having chevron and dog-tooth ornament. These details are part of the church’s historic fabric, alongside traces of earlier work and the changes made in later centuries.

The church has continued to change since the Norman period. The Norbury Chapel and the nineteenth-century alterations are further chapters in the building’s history.
