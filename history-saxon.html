10th-century origins

Saxon traces

The church has 10th-century origins, but its precise foundation date remains uncertain.

The story begins before the Norman Conquest. Historic England identifies 10th-century origins, while describing the surviving west doorway more cautiously as probably of that period.

The parish history suggests a date around 950 for early work. This is an interpretation, not a documented foundation or construction date. The church recorded in Domesday in 1086 gives us a firmer point in the story.

An early trace
