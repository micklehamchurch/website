St Michael and All Angels continues as a place of worship and community life for Mickleham and Westhumble. Its long history belongs to a living parish.

The parish brings together St Michael and All Angels in Mickleham and the Chapel of Ease in Westhumble. Visitors are welcome to discover the church, explore the churchyard and join in parish life.
