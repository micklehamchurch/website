Harvest Family Service

A Harvest of Thankfulness

Sunday 4 October 2026

Autumn arrived beautifully at St Michael's this Sunday, with the church filled with flowers, pumpkins and the rich colours of the season for our Harvest Family Service.

Harvest has long been a time when Christians give thanks for the gifts of the earth and for all those whose work helps to bring food to our tables. But our celebration this year also invited us to think a little more deeply about what having food means in different parts of the world.

During the service, we reflected on the enormous variety of food enjoyed across different countries and cultures. At the same time, we remembered that for many people a full table cannot be taken for granted. In countries affected by war and conflict, families may face uncertainty not only about tomorrow, but about something as simple as their next meal.

That reflection gave an important meaning to our Harvest celebration. Giving thanks for what we have also encourages us to remember others, and reminds us that gratitude and generosity belong together.

There was plenty of joy too. Our younger members helped bring the Family Service to life, while the beautiful Harvest decorations throughout the church provided a wonderful backdrop to our worship.

After the service, the celebration continued with food, conversation and time together. People gathered around the table, caught up with friends and neighbours, and enjoyed the warmth of being together as a church community.

It was a lovely Harvest Sunday in Mickleham — a chance to worship, to give thanks, to think of others and simply to enjoy one another's company.

Thank you to everyone who decorated the church, contributed to the service, prepared and shared food, and helped make the day so special.
