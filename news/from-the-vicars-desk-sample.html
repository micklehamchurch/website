Reflection

From the Vicar’s Desk: making room to listen

SAMPLE DATE · 2 NOVEMBER 2026

A short fictional reflection shows the tone and length a future vicar’s column might use, without attributing it to a real person.

SAMPLE REFLECTION — This is an invented writing example, not a message from the parish vicar or any real member of clergy.

There is a quiet kindness in making room to listen. A shared pause can help neighbours feel seen, and can remind us that community is built through attention as much as through grand gestures.

A future column could offer a brief seasonal thought, a question for reflection or a note of encouragement. The real writer and wording would be supplied and approved by the parish.
