We Will Remember Them

Remembrance Sunday at St Michael & All Angels, Mickleham

On Sunday 8 November, our church and village community came together at St Michael & All Angels to mark Remembrance Sunday — a time to honour those who gave their lives in the service of others, to remember the suffering caused by war, and to pray for peace.

The morning service

The morning began with worship inside our historic church, where the congregation gathered alongside the choir in an atmosphere of reflection and remembrance.

At the war memorial

Following the church service, members of the congregation and wider community gathered outside around Mickleham’s war memorial.

The community observed a two-minute silence, pausing together to remember those who lost their lives in conflict. Poppy wreaths were laid in tribute, a simple but powerful expression of gratitude, remembrance and respect.

Why We Remember

Remembrance Sunday is observed across the United Kingdom on the second Sunday in November, close to the anniversary of the Armistice that ended the fighting of the First World War on 11 November 1918.

Remembering in Mickleham

Our village war memorial stands as a lasting reminder of those connected with this community whose lives were lost in war. Each year, gathering around it brings the history of Mickleham into the present and helps pass the responsibility of remembrance to future generations.

A Moment from the Day

With Thanks

We extend our heartfelt thanks to everyone who joined the service and Act of Remembrance, and to all who helped organise and support the commemoration.
