Norman Architecture

The parish history identifies several Norman features within St Michael and All Angels.

Features described in the parish history

The parish history describes the chancel arch as Norman, with chevron and dog-tooth ornament, and records the chancel lancet windows and stone font as early features. It places much of the present church in the Norman period, around the late twelfth century.

The same historical account describes earlier Saxon traces and later restoration. For context and the full story, see Our History. This page follows the parish account and is not a formal architectural assessment.
