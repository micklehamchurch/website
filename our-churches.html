About our parish

Our Churches

The Parish of Mickleham includes St Michael and All Angels in Mickleham and the Chapel of Ease in Westhumble.

St Michael and All Angels, Mickleham

The parish describes St Michael and All Angels as a historic church at the heart of Mickleham, with Christian worship on the site for more than a thousand years. The church is listed Grade II* according to the old parish website. See Our History for the historical account.

The Chapel of Ease, Westhumble

Westhumble is the parish’s second church. The old website describes the Chapel of Ease as a former barn and notes a much older chapel ruin further up the lane. See the Westhumble Chapel page for the existing account; details should be checked with the parish before a visit.

Part of a wider benefice

The old parish welcome describes Mickleham as part of the United Benefice of Leatherhead and Mickleham within the Diocese of Guildford and the Church of England. Please confirm current organisational wording with the parish.
