Our History

Christian worship has taken place at this site for more than a thousand years. The history of St Michael and All Angels is closely connected with the history of Mickleham itself.

A STORY ACROSS THE CENTURIES

A place of worship through time

Explore key moments in the church’s history, from its earliest traces to parish life today.
Open each period to discover more of the story.

  1. 10th-century origins

    Saxon traces

    The church has 10th-century origins, but its precise foundation date remains uncertain.
  2. 1086

    A church in Domesday

    A written record of a church at Mickleham, alongside two acres of meadow.
  3. Late 12th century

    Norman work

    The Norman chancel arch and font survive within a church much altered in later centuries.
  4. c. 1300

    The Norbury Chapel

    A medieval side chapel with a stone tomb, memorial brasses and connections to Norbury families.
  5. 1823–1891

    Restoration and change

    A succession of 19th-century alterations shaped the church we recognise today.
  6. 1913

    The bells

    A memorial gift of four larger bells completed the peal of six, according to the parish history.
  7. Today

    A living parish

    A continuing place of worship, welcome and community for Mickleham and Westhumble.
