Our Team

The parish is served by clergy, churchwardens, staff and volunteers across worship and community life.

Clergy and parish leadership

The previous team directory listed a Parish Priest, churchwardens and a deputy churchwarden. These roles serve the worshipping community and help care for the parish churches.

Parish administration and PCC

The old directory also listed a Parish Administrator, PCC Secretary and Treasurer, alongside members of the Parochial Church Council. Current office holders and responsibilities should be confirmed by the parish.

Worship and music

The earlier team page named an organist and people serving in worship-related roles. Names and arrangements are not repeated here until confirmed.

Life events

The old directory listed coordinators for baptism, weddings and funerals. Contact the parish for the current enquiry route for each.

Pastoral care and safeguarding

The older team page named a Parish Visitor for pastoral care and a Parish Safeguarding Officer. Current contacts must be confirmed; see the separate Pastoral Care and Safeguarding pages.

Electoral Roll and parish magazine

The previous directory also listed an Electoral Roll Officer and parish magazine editors. Contact the parish to confirm the current role-holders and how to get in touch.
