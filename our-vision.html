Our Vision

Inspired by God’s love, the parish seeks to grow spiritually, make Jesus Christ known and welcome people at the heart of the community.

Inspired by God’s love

The parish vision is: “Inspired by God’s Love we seek to grow spiritually, make Jesus Christ known and be a loving, inclusive and welcoming church, at the heart of the community.”

This vision connects worship, spiritual growth, welcome and service in Mickleham and Westhumble.
