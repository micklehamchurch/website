A parish that cares

Care belongs to the whole parish

In the Parish of Mickleham, people are encouraged to extend pastoral care to one another. It is not only the responsibility of church leaders; everyone has a part to play in reaching out in Christian love.

“I give you a new command: Love one another. Just as I have loved you, you are also to love one another. By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you love one another.”

John 13:34–35

Care in ordinary moments

Here for one another

A close community can help people notice when someone has not been around or when they may need support. The parish describes responding both practically and prayerfully.
