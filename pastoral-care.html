Pastoral Care

The parish seeks to care for one another through spiritual support, practical help and encouragement.

Care within the parish

The parish describes a community where people seek to share one another’s burdens and offer spiritual support, practical help and encouragement. The older team page listed a Parish Visitor role for pastoral care; the named contact may have changed.

If you would like to speak with someone, contact the parish and ask about current pastoral support. The church will respond as it is able; this page does not promise a particular service or response time.

Privacy and safeguarding

The parish says it respects privacy and places importance on confidentiality and safeguarding. Please use the Contact page for current information and share only details needed to make your enquiry.
