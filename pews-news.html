Sun29Nov

Open Doors

Sunday 29th November at 10am in church

PLEASE NOTE: on Sunday 29th November at 10am in church, we will have John Barringer coming to speak on behalf of Open Doors a charity involved in helping persecuted Christians and we will be collecting donations for the charity’s work.

Every day, millions of Christians risk their lives to follow Jesus. In more than 70 countries, Open Doors supports them by supplying Bibles, providing emergency relief and helping persecuted believers stand strong for the long-term. In the UK and Ireland, Open Doors helps the church to pray, give and speak out for those who share our faith but not our freedom.

SAFEGUARDING AUDIT SURVEY NOW OPEN

As part of the Church of England's independent safeguarding audit programme, the Diocese of Guildford and Guildford Cathedral are being audited by safeguarding specialists, INEQE. You can find out more about the audit on the diocesan website.

As part of this process, INEQE wants to hear directly from people across our parish communities about how we create environments where people feel safe, valued and respected.

The audit will help identify strengths in our safeguarding practice, as well as opportunities to learn, improve and build on what we do well.

There are separate survey links for different groups, including clergy and church staff, church members, those who attend parish activities, children and young people, and victims and survivors. The surveys are anonymous and confidential, and we encourage everyone to participate openly and honestly.

Survey links can be found here and will remain open until 1 January 2027.

If you have any questions about the audit, please contact the diocesan safeguarding team at ineqe@cofeguildford.org.uk

You can find out more about the audit on the diocesan website: https://www.cofeguildford.org.uk/ineqe-safeguarding-audit

Survey links can be found here: https://ineqe.com/churchofengland/guildford/
