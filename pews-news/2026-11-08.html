The Nativity Journey at Wintershall - 15th to 20th December 2026

If you have never been to the Wintershall Nativity you may be interested in finding out more about the event. Wintershall is a private estate near Godalming which hosts a re-enactment of the Nativity story.

Attendees can discover the true meaning of Christmas with an unforgettable immersive event for all the family. They stand with the shepherds, walk with Mary, Joseph and the donkey, journey with Roman soldiers and enter the stable to watch the miracle unfold. See the flyer attached to covering email.

Tickets can be booked here: https://www.wintershall.org.uk/event/the-nativity-journey/

A short introductory video is available here: https://youtu.be/xoAFCjrJC88?si=ocGQrvQq3dylr5CN

A message from Liz Absalom

Thank you very much for your support for Macmillan Cancer Research at the Coffee Morning at Chapel Farm last week. I am just about to bank the money raised. This year the total has come to £2,546.50 which is an incredible result. What an amazing team of friends and neighbours you all are. Thank you. I hope that those of you who came along enjoyed the morning and for those of you who couldn’t make it - I hope to see you next year! The money raised from these coffee mornings is vital in allowing Macmillan to continue their work supporting those with cancer in so many ways. Thank you. Please pass on my thanks to anyone who you may have brought with you to the farm last week. It was amazing to see so many people here.
