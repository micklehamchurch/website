Prayer at St Michael’s

The parish’s worship information describes prayer as part of gathering together, alongside Scripture, Holy Communion and fellowship. The church is a place to pause, reflect and bring what is on your heart.

The previous services page listed a quiet service of Compline or Night Prayer on selected Tuesdays. As that timetable may have changed, check the Calendar for current dates or contact the parish.

If you would like to ask for prayer or speak with someone, contact the church. Please share only information you are comfortable communicating by email.
