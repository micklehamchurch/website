Safeguarding

The parish’s safeguarding policy sets out its commitment to a safer church for children, young people and adults.

A safer church

The safeguarding statement published on the previous parish website says the church is committed to promoting a safer environment, safe recruitment and support, responding promptly to concerns, and offering pastoral care to people affected by abuse or allegations.

It also describes working with the incumbent, the Parochial Church Council, the Diocesan Safeguarding Adviser and statutory agencies when concerns arise.

Policy and contacts

The previous policy page said it was agreed in November 2021 and reviewed annually, with the next review stated as July 2026. That review date has passed, so the policy and local contact details need parish confirmation.

Read the existing parish safeguarding statement for the current published policy and contact information. If you have an immediate safeguarding concern, use the appropriate statutory services.
