Worship in the parish

Sunday services

The parish’s previous service guide described a monthly pattern at both churches. Use the Calendar for the dates and times currently listed there; service patterns and arrangements can change.

  • At St Michael and All Angels, the earlier guide described a family service on the first Sunday, Holy Communion on the second, third and fourth Sundays, and Morning Worship on a fifth Sunday.
  • At Westhumble Chapel, it described Book of Common Prayer Holy Communion on the second and fourth Sundays.
  • The guide also mentioned a children’s area and refreshments after the main Mickleham service, and a quiet Compline or Night Prayer on selected Tuesdays. Please check current availability with the parish.
