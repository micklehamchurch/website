Supporting the Community

The parish seeks to serve people in Mickleham and Westhumble through community life and mutual care.

A community of care

The church describes its community life as including practical care, social outreach, courses and pastoral support. The old website does not set out a current directory of support services or partner organisations.

For a current enquiry, contact the parish. The church can let you know what support or signposting is available at present.
