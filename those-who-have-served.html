Gerrard Andrewes D.D. · 1800–1813

Rector · 1800–1813

Gerrard Andrewes D.D.

Their story

Gerrard Andrewes (1750–1825) was educated at Westminster and Trinity College, Cambridge. He was ordained deacon in 1776 and priest in 1778.

During his Mickleham incumbency he also became Rector of St James’s Piccadilly in 1802 and Dean of Canterbury in 1809.

Did you know?

He was offered the Bishopric of Chester in 1812 and declined it.

Alfred Burmester · 1813–1867

Rector · 1813–1867

Alfred Burmester

Their story

Alfred Burmester served as Rector of Mickleham for approximately 54 years. He is buried in a vault beneath the chancel with his wife Jane and two sons who died in infancy.

St Michael’s in their time

His ministry coincided with substantial nineteenth-century rebuilding and restoration at St Michael’s.

Did you know?

Woodwork he obtained in Belgium around 1840 was later incorporated into the church pulpit.

William Henry Harke · 1867–1897

Rector · 1867–1897

William Henry Harke

An 1891 directory records that William Henry Harke had held the Rectory since 1867 and held an M.A. from Trinity College Dublin.

Directory material from this period records a parish population of 799 in 1881 and a National School for 100 children.

Arthur W. Bedford · 1920–1936

Rector · 1920–1936

Arthur W. Bedford

Their story

Local historical material records Sunday services at both Mickleham and Westhumble during Arthur Bedford’s ministry. He was assisted by a curate and retired clergy, including his predecessor William Richard Lloyd.

St Michael’s in their time

In 1929, 27 candidates were presented for confirmation.

Did you know?

A parish account from 1925 describes a water-powered church organ and pressure problems after houses were built in Dell Close.

Rev. Canon John Harkin B.A. · 1993–1998 · 2018–2020

Parish clergy · 1993–1998 · 2018–2020

Rev. Canon John Harkin B.A.

Their story

John Harkin served Mickleham during two separate periods: 1993–1998 and 2018–2020.

Appointment reporting from 1998 identifies him as Priest-in-Charge of Mickleham and Chaplain of Box Hill School when he left for Jersey. Parish material from 2018 describes his return after twenty years.

Rev. Barbara Steadman-Allen B.Mus. · 1999–2004

Parish clergy · 1999–2004

Rev. Barbara Steadman-Allen B.Mus.

Their story

Contemporary material identifies Barbara Steadman-Allen as Priest in Charge around 2000.

When Leatherhead and Mickleham entered a new benefice arrangement in 2002, she continued as the priest responsible for Mickleham while the parishes retained their own PCCs and identities.

Rev. Malcolm Raby B.Ed. · 2013–2017

Parish clergy · 2013–2017

Rev. Malcolm Raby B.Ed.

Their story

Malcolm Raby came to Mickleham in June 2013 as a house-for-duty priest after retiring from full-time ministry as Vicar of Over and Longstanton in the Diocese of Ely.

He lived in the parsonage and undertook parish duties without stipend under the house-for-duty arrangement. Parish material announced his retirement in November 2017.
