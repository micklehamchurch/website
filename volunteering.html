Volunteering

Many gifts and practical contributions help support the life of a church community.

Taking part

The parish describes church life as a shared effort in which people use their time, talents and resources to serve one another and the wider community. The old site does not provide a current list of volunteer roles.

If you would like to offer help, contact the parish to ask what is currently needed. Any role, training, time commitment and safeguarding requirements should be agreed with the church before it begins.
