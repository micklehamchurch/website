Mickleham War Memorial

The war memorial stands in the churchyard at St Michael’s and commemorates local people who died in the two world wars.

Remembering local history

The old parish website says the Mickleham War Memorial is in the churchyard of St Michael’s and bears the names of 11 men who died in the First World War and 11 who died in the Second World War. The Mickleham & Westhumble Local History Group produced an illustrated booklet with information about those commemorated.

The booklet and memorial details are linked from the earlier parish website. Contact the parish or local history group to confirm current access to the booklet.
