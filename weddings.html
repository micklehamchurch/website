A church wedding

The parish welcomes enquiries from people thinking about getting married at St Michael and All Angels. The church’s earlier information directed couples to contact the wedding coordinator.

Eligibility, dates, preparation and fees depend on current Church of England guidance and individual circumstances. Contact the parish to discuss your situation and confirm present arrangements.
