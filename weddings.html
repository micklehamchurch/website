A wedding at St Michael’s

The parish welcomes enquiries from couples considering a wedding at St Michael and All Angels. The local parish is the place to begin a conversation about your circumstances and what may be possible.

Planning your service

The parish can discuss the next steps with you. Eligibility, dates, preparation and fees depend on current Church of England guidance and your circumstances, so they should be confirmed directly with the parish.

Creating beauty, sustainably

For regular and occasional services, the parish encourages seasonal, local flowers and foliage and asks that floral foam (including Oasis) is not used. Flowers do best arranged in water; the guidance links to Sustainable Church Flowers for sustainable mechanics and inspiration. Flower use in church is discouraged during Advent and Lent. If you are planning a wedding in either season, please ask the Wedding Co-ordinator what may be appropriate.

For a special occasion or information about local providers, contact Alison Wood at flowers@micklehamchurch.org.uk or 01372 376443.

Church of England wedding guidance

The Church of England’s national guidance offers further information about eligibility, banns, the ceremony and practical preparations. For local questions, contact the parish.
