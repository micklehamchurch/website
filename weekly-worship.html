Worship with us

You are welcome to worship with us at St Michael and All Angels, Mickleham, and at the Chapel of Ease in Westhumble.

Sunday worship at Mickleham

The current parish Calendar lists Sunday worship at 10.00 am at St Michael and All Angels. It includes monthly All-Age Worship and Holy Communion on other Sundays shown in the Calendar. Each event listing gives the service details for that date.

Westhumble Chapel

The Calendar currently lists Book of Common Prayer Holy Communion at the Chapel of Ease, Westhumble, on the second and fourth Sundays of the month at 8.00 am. Please check the Calendar before travelling, as it is the current source for dates and service details.
