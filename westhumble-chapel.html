Westhumble

The Chapel of Ease

The Chapel of Ease in Westhumble is the second church in the Parish of Mickleham.

The earlier parish history describes the chapel building as a former barn and refers to the ruins of an older chapel further up the lane, thought in that account to have been built around 1200 for a tenant of Merton Priory.

These historical notes come from the previous parish website. Check the Calendar for current services, and contact the parish to confirm access before travelling.
