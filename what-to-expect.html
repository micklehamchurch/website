What to Expect

You are welcome to visit, take your time and join in with worship as much or as little as feels comfortable.

A warm welcome

The parish welcomes visitors to its services and activities. You may take time to settle in, sit quietly, and ask someone if you need help.

Service formats vary. Check the Calendar for current service details, and contact the parish if you have a question about what to expect or need to confirm practical arrangements.
